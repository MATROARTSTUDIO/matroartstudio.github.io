About Us

The Studio Behind
India's Most Important Events

MATRO ART STUDIO is Delhi's premier event planning and management company, based in Lajpat Nagar. Since our founding in 2008, we've been the trusted partner for India's most important institutions — delivering memorable, precisely-executed experiences at the highest levels.

We specialize in government conferences, ministerial meetings, national summits, and high-profile corporate events. Our portfolio spans 100+ Ministries and government bodies, with 1000+ events executed flawlessly across the country.

Who We Are
Our Story
Since 2008

Delhi's Most Trusted
Event Partners

MATRO ART STUDIO was founded in 2008 with a singular vision — to bring world-class event management to India's corridors of power. Based in Lajpat Nagar, New Delhi, we have grown from a passionate startup to the most trusted name in government event management.

Over 18 years, we have built deep expertise in the unique demands of government protocol, media management, and large-scale conference organization. Our clients include 100+ Ministries and premier constitutional bodies of the Government of India.

What sets us apart is not just our capability — it's our integrity, precision, and unwavering commitment to excellence. Every event we touch reflects our belief that the moments we create become part of India's institutional memory.
