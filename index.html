The Studio Behind
India's Most Important Events

MATRO ART STUDIO is Delhi's premier event planning and management company, based in Lajpat Nagar. Since our founding in 2008, we've been the trusted partner for India's most important institutions — delivering memorable, precisely-executed experiences at the highest levels.

We specialize in government conferences, ministerial meetings, national summits, and high-profile corporate events. Our portfolio spans 100+ Ministries and government bodies, with 1000+ events executed flawlessly across the country.
