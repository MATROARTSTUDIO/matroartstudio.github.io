Why Choose Us

The Most Trusted Event Partner for Government of India

When government ministries and PSUs need a reliable event management company in New Delhi, they choose Matro Art Studio. Our deep understanding of government protocols, security requirements, and procurement processes — combined with 18+ years of on-ground experience — makes us the go-to agency for high-stakes events.
